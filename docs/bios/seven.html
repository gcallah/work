Contributor

Gülşen Seven

TED University

Gülşen Seven is an Assistant Professor of Political Science and International Relations at TED University, where she teaches history of political thought and contemporary political theory. She holds a PhD in Political Science and Public Administration from Bilkent University. Her research interests include realism and moralism in political theory, the concept of political judgement and the relationship between political theory and political practice.
