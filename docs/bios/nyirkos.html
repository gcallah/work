Contributor

Tamás Nyirkos

Research Institute for Politics and Government, University of Public Service, Budapest

Tamás Nyirkos Is a research fellow at the Research Institute for Politics and Government, University of Public Service, Budapest, and associate professor of political science at the Pázmány Péter Catholic University since 2012. He earned his PhD in moral and political philosophy from Eötvös Loránd University in 2011. His most significant work is “The Tyranny of the Majority,” published by Routledge in 2018.
