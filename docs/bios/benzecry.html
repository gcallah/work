Contributor

Gabriel F. Benzecry

Middle Tennessee State University

Gabriel Benzecry is a third-year Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU). He is the recipient of the PERI Ph.D. Fellowship for the 2022 academic year. He recieved his B.A. in Economics at Wake Forest University. Gabriel’s research focuses on Political Economy and the History of Ideas. He currently works as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for the Jones College of Business, teatching Principles of Microeconomics and Macroeconomics.
