By the Sweat of Your Brow

The Concept of Work in the History of European Philosophy

Is work a curse or a calling, the enemy of leisure or a basic human good? Twenty-one scholars trace how the Western tradition has understood work, from Plato, Aristotle and the New Testament through Aquinas, Luther, Locke, Smith, Hegel and Marx to Weber, Arendt, Pieper, Oakeshott and MacIntyre.

By the sweat of your brow you will eat your food until you return to the ground.Genesis 3:19
