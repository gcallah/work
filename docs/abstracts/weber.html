Chapter 12 · Abstract

The Protestant Hypothesis and the Spirit of Weber

Gene CallahanNew York University

Max Weber famously explained the rise of capitalism by pointing to the Protestant ethic, which, he claimed, was a new attitude towards work. A number of scholars have since disputed his explanation. This paper will examine the critiques, and see if Weber can answer them.
